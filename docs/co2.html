Where emissions concentrate (spatial → scatter over a real France outline)

This decision is about geography (which region to target), so a plain ranking is not enough. Each city is plotted at its real coordinates, colored by its CO2 contribution, over a simplified outline of France. Existing warehouses get their own marker shape (a triangle), not just a color, because a shape survives grayscale printing and color-blindness. It also answers a second question at a glance: is CO2 concentrated near a warehouse (fixable by re-routing) or far from every warehouse (fixable only by opening a new one)?
