Comparing scenarios (bar chart of discrete options, not a line)

These four configurations are discrete, mutually exclusive choices, not points on a continuum. A line chart would wrongly suggest you can interpolate between them. One bar per scenario, sorted best-to-worst, answers the question directly.

Why: baseline vs. the best alternative (side-by-side maps)

This decision is about geographic coverage more than either of the other two dashboards, so a spatial view is the main evidence here. Each city is plotted over a France outline and colored by the warehouse it is assigned to, so the coverage shift is visible at a glance. The warehouse city itself is drawn as a bigger, black-outlined triangle inside its own color group: color shows "which warehouse serves this city", shape shows "which point is the actual warehouse".
